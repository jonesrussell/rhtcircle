# RHT Circle content review, triage and initial seeding

Date: July 26, 2026

## Editorial finding

RHT Circle has the technical skeleton of a 21-Nation publication, but its content has been operating like a strong Sagamok accountability project attached to a much thinner Treaty-wide reference site.

Before this pass:

- all 21 Nations had a sourced profile;
- Sagamok had 17 member-transparency cards, four territory cards and two community-life cards;
- Garden River had one member-record card;
- Atikameksheng, Serpent River and Sagamok shared one community-life feature;
- 12 Nation pages had no Nation-specific community-life, land, reporting or member-work card;
- the news digest had no community tags and did not feed stories back to community pages;
- the contributor invitation described only one role, a local transparency contact.

The content model now treats every Nation page as a community desk. Each page leads with the Nation's own website, adds reviewed Nation-specific reporting where available, and then adds current Treaty-wide reporting. The news page links stories back to the relevant communities and exposes all 21 desks.

## Verified initial seeds

### Whitefish River and Sagamok: regional long-term care exploration

The June 2026 announcement says Whitefish River First Nation, Sagamok Anishnawbek, the Town of Espanola and the Espanola Regional Hospital and Health Centre are exploring the possibility of a regional long-term care home. It is an exploratory partnership, not evidence that a facility, location, financing or operating agreement is final.

Sources:

- https://www.whitefishriver.ca/news
- https://www.espanolaregionalhospital.ca/news-and-community/news/first-of-its-kind-partnership-to-transform-long-term-care-in-northern-ontario
- https://anishinabeknews.ca/2026/06/first-of-its-kind-partnership-to-transform-long-term-care-in-northern-ontario/

### Wiikwemkoong: Elders Home

Wiikwemkoong's public infrastructure series identifies a new Elders Home as one of its 2024–2026 projects. The Nursing Home redevelopment page describes the existing 59-bed facility, the need for a replacement and a 96-bed model. The RHT Circle seed links to the Nation's current updates rather than treating a contributor screenshot as the source.

Sources:

- https://www.wiikwemkoong.ca/tag/updates/
- https://www.wikwemikongnursinghome.com/redevelopment-update/
- https://www.ontario.ca/locations/longtermcare/homes/1856-wikwemikong-nursing-home/

### Shawanaga: water treatment

On July 23, 2026, Shawanaga and Indigenous Services Canada announced the new water treatment plant. The release says the centralized system is designed to serve 101 homes and 10 community buildings and describes approximately $31 million in combined public funding. It also says the long-term drinking-water advisory can be lifted by Chief and Council once distribution-system expansion is complete.

Source:

- https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/shawanaga-first-nation-marks-grand-opening-of-new-water-treatment-plant-874464381.html

### Atikameksheng: boundary claim

The January 2026 RHT Litigation Fund notice says Treaty leadership directed an intervention in Atikameksheng's boundary case because the outcome may affect Treaty-wide harvesting rights and future annuity calculations.

## Contributor model

The site should support five overlapping roles:

1. Community steward: keeps one Nation page current.
2. Beat contributor: follows one subject across Nations.
3. Writer: develops reporting, commentary or explainers.
4. Source reviewer: checks records, dates, links and wording.
5. Local Circle contact: helps members use the shared transparency standard.

A contributor can use a public byline, an agreed name, no public byline, or begin privately. Nothing is represented as their work until they review the final page.

## Seed queue

### P0: correctness and expiring information

- Recheck the 11 profiles carrying explicit review notes.
- Replace stale population figures with one consistent federal snapshot and record the snapshot date.
- Recheck every safety-service phone number and link.
- Verify governance and election-system descriptions against each Nation's own public pages.

### P1: balanced community depth

- Establish at least one Nation-specific current item for every desk.
- Invite Whitefish River and Wiikwemkoong contributors to define the Elders, health and housing beat.
- Build durable topic pages for Elders and health, housing and infrastructure, land and water, language and culture, youth, governance, and settlement decisions.
- Add an editorial corrections page and contributor guide.

### P2: discovery and publishing workflow

- Add topic, Nation and author archives.
- Add site search.
- Move reviewed stories and updates from a hand-coded feed into managed content with Nation and topic relationships.
- Add an editorial status workflow: lead, checking, draft, contributor review, ready, published, update required.

## Editorial rule for seeding

An official Nation link is the first card on every desk. Outside reporting is summarized, dated and linked. Nation-specific stories appear before Treaty-wide stories. A contributor's first-hand account can guide reporting, but it does not become a site fact until it is either clearly presented as their account or supported by a public source.
